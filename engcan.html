The International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) is an official system of naming, classifying, and representing sounds in any language. Learning it is a vital step in being able to identify sounds visually and auditorily.

Vowels

Vowels are sounded created by keeping your mouth open. The sound of vowels depend on 3 factors: where you position your tongue in your mouth, how vertically stressed your lip muscles are, and whether your round your lips into an O shape. Below is a visual guide on how different vowel sounds are formed. Not all combination of factors may produce a pronuncable vowel.
