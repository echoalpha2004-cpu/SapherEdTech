About Us

Welcome to Sapher, a resource specifically designed for language learners to learn pronouncing sounds through interactive and techincal tools that teach sounds on a fundamental level.

Our Mission

We believe that pronunciation, despite often being overlooked in formal language education, is an important aspect of communication and connection with others. Our aim is to promote pronunciation literacy and bolster acquisition among speakers.
