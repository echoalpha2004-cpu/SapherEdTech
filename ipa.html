Consonants

Consonants are sounds created by keeping your mouth mostly closed, and creating physical contact between parts of your mouth such as your tongue, teeth, and lips. Below is a diagram of different parts of the mouth and the different sounds they produce.

Vowels

Vowels are sounded created by keeping your mouth open. The sound of vowels depend on 3 factors: where you position your tongue in your mouth, how vertically stressed your lip muscles are, and whether your round your lips into an O shape.
